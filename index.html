Reading this map

Two layers over the same suburbs, each switched on or off above. Operators (blue) counts accredited businesses. Mass vehicles (orange) counts the powered units they currently nominate, which is the closer read on demand. Both use one radius scale, so where the orange reaches well past the blue the operators there run large fleets. Vehicles match to operators by accreditation number for 92 per cent of records; a hollow ring marks a suburb whose operators have no matching vehicle record. Coverage is Victoria, South Australia and Tasmania; New South Wales and Queensland appear in single figures, so this extract does not describe the national scheme.
